Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 30, 2026

What we collect

When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and profile picture from Google. That's it.

Why we collect it

To create your account, display your chosen display name on the leaderboard, and let you track your typing stats across sessions. Your email is never shown publicly.

Storage

Your account info (name, email, Google ID, display name) is stored in our database. Your game scores, typing stats, and level progression are stored and linked to your account.

Your session token is stored in an HttpOnly, SameSite browser cookie that expires after 7 days, so your login survives closing or opening tabs during play while staying inaccessible to page JavaScript. A small public profile cache (display name/avatar info, not the secret token) is stored in localStorage for fast UI startup and is cleared on sign-out or when the server rejects the session.

Sharing

We do not share your data with anyone. No third-party analytics, no ad networks, no data brokers. Your email is never sold, traded, or disclosed.

Deletion

To delete your account and all associated data, send a request to the contact email listed on this page. Alternatively, you can revoke KeyboardRage access from your Google Account settings at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
